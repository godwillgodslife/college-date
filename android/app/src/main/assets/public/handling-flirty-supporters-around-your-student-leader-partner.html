Protecting Mental Bandwidth, Emotional Resilience, and Academic Priorities

Your Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) remains your highest personal asset as an undergraduate. When interpersonal misunderstandings or emotional turmoil consume your attention, continuous assessments and examination results inevitably suffer.

Holding student office while managing relationship expectations and preparing for rigorous semester examinations creates acute cognitive overload. Political smear campaigns, aggressive social media attacks, and contentious union politics can trigger anxiety and sleep deprivation, directly threatening student CGPAs.
