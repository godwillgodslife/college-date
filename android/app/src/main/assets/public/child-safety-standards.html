The College Date Child Safety Standards

Last updated: May 13, 2026

The College Date is committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on our platform. Our app is intended for adult users, and we maintain safety practices for reporting, reviewing, and removing harmful content or behavior.

Prohibited Content And Conduct

We do not allow content, accounts, messages, images, videos, links, or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, grooms, solicits, traffics, or otherwise harms minors. This includes sharing, requesting, threatening to share, or promoting CSAM or CSAE.

Reporting

Users can report safety concerns in the app where reporting tools are available. Child safety concerns can also be sent directly to our safety contact: godwillgodslife@gmail.com.

Review And Enforcement

We review reported safety concerns and may remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts, preserve relevant records, or take other enforcement action where needed. Accounts involved in child exploitation or abuse are not permitted on The College Date.

Authority Reporting

Where required by applicable law, we report apparent CSAM or child exploitation concerns to the appropriate regional or national authorities and cooperate with lawful requests.

Contact

Designated child safety contact: godwillgodslife@gmail.com.
