1. Purpose and Scope

The College Date (Application ID: com.collegedate.app), developed and published by Neway Marketing Enterprises, is dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of safety, dignity, and integrity across all digital surfaces.

We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form. Although The College Date is exclusively intended for adult undergraduate and postgraduate students aged 18 and older in tertiary institutions, we enforce rigorous automated and human safeguards to prevent, detect, eradicate, and report any attempt to exploit or endanger children.

2. Explicit Prohibition of CSAE and CSAM

The College Date strictly prohibits any user from creating, uploading, possessing, distributing, transmitting, soliciting, facilitating, or promoting:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Visual depictions, images, video recordings, audio recordings, digital illustrations, or synthetic/AI-generated representations depicting any person under 18 years of age engaged in sexually explicit conduct or depicted in a sexually suggestive or exploitative manner.
  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE): Any act or communication involving the grooming of minors, enticement, sexual solicitation of a minor, child sextortion, financial exploitation of a minor, or commercial sexual exploitation and trafficking.
  • Underage Accounts: Any account created by, depicting, or impersonating an individual under the age of 18.
  • Links & External Referrals to CSAE: Sharing URLs, file-sharing links, social media handles, or cloud drives that host, distribute, or facilitate access to CSAM or CSAE.

Any violation of these standards will result in immediate and permanent expulsion, hardware/device ban, and mandatory reporting to law enforcement authorities.

3. Strict Age Requirement (18+ Platform)

The College Date is an adult community tailored exclusively for enrolled university, polytechnic, and college students. During onboarding, users must:

  • Affirm that they are at least 18 years old.
  • Complete mandatory university / tertiary institution selection.
  • Complete mandatory AI-assisted selfie facial verification to ensure that account holders are authentic adult students.

If an account is identified as belonging to or representing an individual under 18, it is immediately suspended, all associated data is quarantined, and access to the service is revoked.

4. Integrated In-App Reporting & Feedback Mechanism (Without Leaving the App)

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policy, The College Date provides a dedicated, functional in-app mechanism for user feedback and child safety reporting that operates entirely within the app without requiring users to leave or open an external web browser or email client.

Users have two direct, native pathways to submit reports and feedback within the app:

Pathway A: In-Chat & Profile User Reporting

  1. Inside any chat or profile conversation, tap the three dots (⋮) menu icon in the top header.
  2. Select 🚩 Report User.
  3. Select 🛡️ Child Safety / Underage Account (specifically designated for suspected minors, CSAE, or CSAM).
  4. Optionally add additional context and choose whether to block the user.
  5. Tap Submit Report. The report is transmitted instantly and confidential in-app confirmation is provided immediately.

Pathway B: Global In-App Feedback & Safety Concern Modal (Settings)

  1. Open the Settings screen in The College Date.
  2. Navigate to the Support & Feedback section.
  3. Tap 🛡️ Report Child Safety Concern or 💬 Send In-App Feedback.
  4. An in-app modal opens directly over the screen, allowing users to select the 🛡️ Child Safety / Underage Concern (Priority) category.
  5. Enter details of the concern, optional target username, and optional contact email.
  6. Tap Submit Urgent Report. An immediate receipt ticket (e.g. TCD-XXXXXXXX) and status confirmation are displayed directly inside the app without leaving.

All reports submitted through these in-app mechanisms are routed to our high-priority moderation queue for immediate human triage.

5. Proactive Detection, Moderation & Technical Safeguards

The College Date employs defense-in-depth safety engineering to prevent prohibited material from entering the platform:

  • Automated Text & Contact Guards: Real-time filtering scans bios, messages, and campus confessions for grooming language, off-platform contact sharing, and phone/fintech proxies.
  • AI Visual Verification: All uploaded profile photos are scanned via computer vision pipelines before public display to detect nudity, inappropriate content, and underage facial signatures.
  • View-Once Ephemeral Protections: Direct media exchanges in chat employ secure ephemeral tokenization and strict access gates.
  • Device Fingerprinting & Re-Registration Blocks: Accounts banned for child safety violations are permanently blacklisted using hardware hashes, preventing perpetrators from opening new accounts.

6. Enforcement Procedures & Mandatory Law Enforcement Cooperation

Upon receiving a child safety report or identifying apparent CSAE/CSAM:

  1. Emergency Priority Triage: Child safety reports are reviewed within 1 hour of receipt by our designated Child Safety Officer.
  2. Immediate Content Removal: The reported content and associated messages are immediately removed from all public feeds and encrypted messaging threads.
  3. Account Termination: The offending user's account is immediately and permanently terminated. All active sessions, tokens, and access privileges are invalidated.
  4. Evidence Preservation: Relevant logs, IP addresses, transaction records, and cryptographic hashes are securely preserved in accordance with applicable legal evidentiary standards.
  5. NCMEC CyberTipline Reporting: In compliance with international child protection standards, instances of CSAM or child exploitation are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline.
  6. Law Enforcement Escalation: We proactively cooperate with national and international authorities, including the Nigeria Police Force (NPF) Cybercrime Division, the National Agency for the Prohibition of Trafficking in Persons (NAPTIP), and INTERPOL.
