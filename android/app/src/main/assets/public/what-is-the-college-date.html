What Is The College Date?

The College Date is a campus dating and student social discovery app for students who want to meet, match, chat, and connect around school communities. The official website is thecollegedate.com.

Short Answer

The College Date is built for campus-focused discovery: student profiles, matching, chat, premium discovery features, and public safety guidance. It is also searched as TheCollegeDate, College Date, CollegeDate, TCD, CD app, and thecollegedate.
