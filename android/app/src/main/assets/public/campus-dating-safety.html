Campus Dating Safety

The College Date is designed for student connections, and a stronger community starts with clear safety expectations.

Respectful Student Connections

Students should use The College Date to meet and communicate respectfully. Harassment, impersonation, exploitation, abuse, or unsafe behavior does not belong in the community.

Reporting Concerns

Users should report safety concerns, suspicious profiles, abusive messages, or child safety concerns through the available in-app reporting and support channels. Serious concerns may be escalated according to applicable law and platform policies.

Meeting In Person

Students should choose public places, tell someone they trust, and avoid sharing sensitive personal or financial information with people they have just met.
