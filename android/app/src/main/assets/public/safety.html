Safety on The College Date

The College Date is intended for adult students and campus communities. Safety here means clear rules, responsible profiles, cautious matching, support contact paths, privacy choices, and guidance that helps users make better decisions before meeting offline.

What is verified about the platform

The current app includes signup, onboarding, age entry with an 18+ check in profile setup, school and level fields, interests, dating intention, profile photos, mutual discovery, chat, notification settings, profile visibility status, online-status controls, incognito mode, account deletion information, and public safety pages.

The app also includes AI profile trust checks in parts of the product. That should be described cautiously as a trust-support feature, not as a guarantee that every account is identity-verified or background-checked.

User responsibilities

Users are responsible for accurate profile information, respectful communication, and safe decisions when moving from chat to real life. Do not impersonate others, scam users, harass people, share non-consensual intimate material, exploit minors, spam, or pressure someone after they decline.

The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy explain expected conduct, data handling, payments, deletion, and support contacts.

Reporting and Support (In-App Mechanism)

The College Date provides an integrated in-app mechanism for reporting and user feedback without leaving the app. Inside any chat conversation or profile, tap the three dots (⋮) and select Report User (with a dedicated category for Child Safety / Underage Account). Users can also open Settings → Support & Feedback to submit direct feedback or report child safety concerns at any time.

Our official Child Safety Standards are published in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policy. The platform is strictly for adults (18+), and we maintain a zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Reports are reviewed by our designated Child Safety Officer (childsafety@thecollegedate.com).

Offline meeting guidance

For first meetings, choose public, visible places, tell a trusted person, keep your phone charged, arrange your own transport, and leave if you feel uncomfortable. Do not share bank details, passwords, ID documents, exact room location, or private media with someone you do not fully trust.

For step-by-step offline planning, read How to Date Safely on Campus in Nigeria. For broader student education, read Campus Dating Safety.

What this page does not claim

This page does not claim universal identity verification, guaranteed student verification, background checks, continuous human monitoring, guaranteed safety, or university endorsement. Those claims should not appear anywhere unless the owner provides verifiable evidence and implementation support.

FAQs

Is The College Date for people under 18?

No. The public positioning is for adult students, and onboarding requires users to be at least 18.

Does The College Date guarantee safety?

No online or offline dating service can guarantee safety. The platform provides rules, support paths, trust features, and guidance, while users must still make careful decisions.

How do I contact support?

Use the Support page, email info@thecollegedate.com, or contact the published safety email for safety concerns.
