The College Date

The College Date, also written as TheCollegeDate and sometimes searched as College Date, CollegeDate, TCD, or CD app, is a campus dating and student social discovery app for students who want to meet, match, chat, and connect around school communities.

What The College Date Means

The College Date is not a college admissions service or academic planning website. It is a dating and social discovery platform focused on campus communities, student connections, mutual matching, chat, and safer online dating guidance.
