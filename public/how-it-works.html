How The College Date Works

The College Date is built for the rhythm, confidence, and culture of campus life. Here is the step-by-step walkthrough of how verified Nigerian students discover, match, and connect.

The Four Steps

01

Create Your Profile

Register with your Nigerian university, department, level, and lifestyle interests. Our Vision AI validates your profile photo to ensure real student accounts.

02

Discover Around Campus

Browse student cards in your school or explore neighboring institutions. Filter by study dates, exam-crunch partners, serious relationships, or casual campus gist.

03

Match with Mutual Intent

Like someone's profile with zero pressure. When they like you back, you are instantly matched. Mutual matching prevents unsolicited spam and ensures both students want to talk.

04

Chat & Build Real Connections

Exchange high-clarity voice notes, view-once photos, assignment documents, and playful campus gifts (Cold Zobo, Suya). Take it from the screen to a safe campus hangout.

Platform Capabilities

📍 Live Location Sharing for Safe Meetups

When meeting a match on campus for the first time, share a 15-minute, 30-minute, or 1-hour live GPS pin inside chat to easily spot each other at the library or faculty cafeteria.

🛡️ Safe & Confidential Campus Moderation

Strict 18+ eligibility, biometric login unlock, disappearing message timers (24h, 7d, 30d), and one-tap confidential reporting to protect our student community.

🤝 Service Deals & Assignment Escrow

Need a course-mate to tutor you or help with a design project? Agree on deliverables inside chat with built-in student wallet escrow protection.

Campus Meeting Safety

Always prioritize your peace of mind. Meet during daylight in public campus spots like university libraries, faculty walks, and central cafeterias.
