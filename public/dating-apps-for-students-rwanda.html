Safety Standards and Campus Etiquette in Rwanda

Kigali's high public safety standards make campus dating pleasant and secure when following basic common sense:

  1. Meet in Populated Public Spaces: Choose reputable, well-lit daytime spots like campus cafeterias, student centre coffee shops, or popular public cafes in Kigali (e.g. Kimihurura or Remera). Avoid private apartments on a first meeting.
  2. Never Send Money Before Meeting: Genuine student connections never involve financial requests. Refuse any requests for mobile transfers or airtime.
  3. Maintain Independent Transportation: Arrange your own transport to and from the meetup, whether by public bus or trusted moto-taxi.
  4. Respect Personal Boundaries: Mutual consent, polite conversation, and clear communication are essential for healthy campus dating.
