How to Date Safely on Campus in Nigeria

Dating should feel exciting, not unsafe. Whether you meet through an app, a classmate, a friend group, or a campus event, the same safety habits matter.

Protect Private Information

Do not share passwords, banking details, private addresses, personal documents, or sensitive photos. A real connection will not pressure you for private information early.

Meet Publicly First

If you decide to meet offline, choose a public, familiar place. Tell a trusted friend where you are going, keep your phone charged, and leave if anything feels wrong.

Respect Boundaries

No one owes a reply, a date, a call, money, photos, or access to their personal life. Good campus dating starts with respect and consent.

Use Report and Block Tools

If someone harasses, scams, threatens, impersonates, or sends harmful content, stop engaging and use the app's safety tools. The College Date also publishes public safety information for users and partners.
