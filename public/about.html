About The College Date

The College Date is a campus dating and social app built for students who want to meet people around their school community.

What The App Does

Students can create a profile, discover other students, send likes, match with people who like them back, and start conversations through chat. The app is designed for campus-focused dating, social discovery, and meaningful student connections.

Mission

The College Date exists to make student discovery feel more relevant than a broad dating app. The product focuses on campus context, clearer profiles, mutual matching, private chat, safety reporting, and public guidance for students who want to meet other students responsibly.

The public website supports that mission by publishing official brand facts, safety standards, privacy information, download guidance, and educational content for university, polytechnic, and college students across Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, Ghana, and higher education across the African continent for adults aged 18 and above.

Whether navigating residence life at UCT in Cape Town, commuter campuses in Lagos, student hostels in Nairobi, or hall culture in Kumasi, The College Date is built to reflect the authentic energy, humor, and lifestyle of African students.

Student Verification and Safety Context

The College Date is positioned for adult students and campus communities. User profiles, photos, and account information help create context before people match or chat. The app also publishes safety guidance, child safety standards, privacy information, and account deletion instructions so students and partners can review how the service presents trust and support information.
