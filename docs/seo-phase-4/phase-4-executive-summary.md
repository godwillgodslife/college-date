## Cannibalization Issues Discovered

- Dating app in Nigeria cluster has overlapping pages; assign /dating-app-in-nigeria as the definitive commercial page.
- Student dating, campus dating, and college dating app pages must have differentiated intent.
- Safety pages should separate trust hub, campus safety cluster, and practical guide intent.
- Tool pages must not duplicate their supporting articles; tools own interactive intent.

## Business Facts Needed From Owner

- Exact student verification workflow and what claims can be made publicly.
- Current Google Play status, screenshots, and approved store assets.
- Any official campus partnerships or ambassador programs, if they exist.
- Support response process and reporting workflow details.
- Any real founder/company registration details that should be public.

## Briefs to Hold Until More Facts Are Available

- Any university-specific landing page naming a school.
- Any page claiming verified student status beyond what the app can prove.
- Any testimonial, rating, user-count, or partnership page.
- Any legal/medical/mental-health page requiring qualified expert review.

## Recommended Next Phase

Phase 5 should produce the first 12 foundation pages from the briefs, starting with expansion of existing pages before creating new pages. Build no interactive tools until their support articles and safety review patterns exist.
