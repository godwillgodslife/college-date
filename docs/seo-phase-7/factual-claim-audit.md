# Factual Claim Audit

Audit date: 2026-07-21.

## Result

Passed with no unsupported claims found by the Phase 7 static regression gate. No reviews, aggregate ratings, fabricated awards, university affiliations, fake user counts, or unsupported safety guarantees were added in Phase 7.

## Claim Controls Preserved

- Review and AggregateRating schema remain blocked unless backed by evidence.
- Safety pages avoid guaranteed safety, background-check, or universal verification claims.
- App and AI claims remain framed as product features, not outcome guarantees.
- Comparison and commercial pages retain publisher transparency and do not claim objective market leadership.

## Owner Confirmation Still Needed

- Any future claims about verified student coverage by institution.
- Any usage numbers, success rates, ratings, awards, partnerships, or campus endorsements.
- Any new privacy or safety capability not visible in code or public policy.
