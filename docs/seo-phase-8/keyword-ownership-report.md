- /red-flags-student-relationships is the companion to /green-flags-student-relationships and should not target positive healthy-relationship signs as primary.
- /healthy-relationship-university-students targets starting a relationship; /student-dating remains the broader life-stage pillar.
- /talking-stage-students targets pre-relationship communication; /situationship-vs-relationship targets definition and commitment clarity.
- /how-to-reject-someone-politely targets refusal scripts and boundaries; /student-dating-etiquette targets broader behavior standards.
- /breakup-recovery-college-students targets recovery and wellbeing; it should not be merged into general student dating.
